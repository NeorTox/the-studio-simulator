PLAYER HANDBOOK

How to play

A practical guide to running your studio and protecting your save.

PLAYABLE ALPHA
01 · Start a campaign

Open Campaigns and create a new save. Choose any date from 1800 onward, then define your founder, education, attributes, personal cash, company name, location, legal structure, currency and opening capital.

02 · Build the company

Incorporate the company, add divisions, hire employees, acquire facilities, insure operations and build your IP catalogue. Company money and founder personal money are separate.

03 · Develop productions

Create a project, then move it through concept, development, pre-production, photography, post-production, marketing and release. Each stage has a minimum time requirement. Production spending is allocated from the approved budget; cast and crew contracts are commitments against that budget.

04 · Cast and crew

Launch auditions at local, regional, national or international scale. Review generated fictional applicants, add talent to your roster and sign casting contracts. Hire project crew separately.

05 · Marketing and distribution

Create an era-appropriate marketing plan during post-production. At release, enter gross receipts, exhibitor/platform share, distributor fee, marketing costs and delivery costs. Gross receipts are not the same as studio revenue or profit.

06 · Awards and recognition

Submit released projects to awards and festivals that exist in the campaign year. Results are simulated from recorded critical and audience scores, company reputation and judging variance.

07 · Time and recurring costs

The clock is manual. Advance one day, week, month or year. Month-end payroll, facility rent, maintenance, insurance and scheduled loan instalments are charged when due. Fixed assets depreciate monthly; configured tax is accrued on positive pre-tax profit. If cash is insufficient for mandatory cash obligations, time stops before the due date.

08 · Finance and ownership

Finance includes a double-entry journal, accrual invoices, accounts receivable and payable, partial settlements, overdue tracking, fixed-asset depreciation, amortizing term loans, configurable simulated tax accruals, marketable-securities trading and a ledger-derived management balance sheet. Run the integrity audit to reconcile cash, debt, invoices, assets, depreciation and tax control accounts. Raise private equity, acquire competitors, or complete an IPO. Public share prices update on trading days.

Saves and backups

Campaigns are saved in this browser on this device. Use Campaigns to switch saves, export a JSON backup or import one. Export backups regularly. Cloud accounts and cross-device synchronization are not connected in this alpha.

Sandbox and achievements

Sandbox mode starts with achievements disabled. Executing any successful cheat disables achievements permanently for that save and records the command in the audit log. Other saves are unaffected.

Simulation precision & calibration

Core economic calculations use 12-decimal fixed-point arithmetic (BigInt-backed) to avoid binary floating-point drift. A deterministic world seed produces repeatable market shocks. Macroeconomic indicators update monthly; equities trade on simulated business days. Prices, returns and index values are stored at high precision, while the interface rounds them for readability. This is a calibrated simulation model, not a claim of exact real-world forecasting.

What this build does not yet simulate completely

This is a playable alpha, not a finished commercial game. Full historical event datasets, real-time real-person availability, tax and statutory accounting, cloud accounts, detailed legal jurisdiction rules, union agreements, complete hostile-takeover mechanics, global subsidiary regulation, AI research, cybersecurity incidents and autonomous character life events are still planned.
